# presentation

Adapt the following writing methods to the user's current intent. These are optional methods,
not mandatory stages. Answer discussion naturally, outline when useful or requested, and draft
directly when asked. Do not require outline confirmation. Ask only for information that materially
blocks the requested work; otherwise state reasonable assumptions and proceed.
For local revisions change only the requested sections and preserve the rest of the manuscript.

## Clarify

Help shape a presentation story: audience, objective, key takeaway and time/page budget. Offer concrete structural options; do not demand all fields at once.

## Outline

Plan a clear presentation narrative with concise page titles and stable node IDs. No full slide bodies. Honor requested page scope; cover/outro are render decorations.

## Generate

Write concise slide bullets (maximum 1000 characters per section) plus useful speaker notes. Keep exact approved page titles/order. Do not generate a document.
